VELVET HOUR

A museum stealth caper in four galleries.

Midnight. The museum sleeps, its guards patrol, and four treasures sit in glass cases with your name on them. Slip past the vision cones, keep to the burgundy rugs where the light cannot follow, lift each gallery's prize — and walk out of the green doors unseen.

  • Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Hold Shift to sneak: slower, silent, harder to see.
  • Amber cones are guard vision — they redden as suspicion climbs. At ? a guard stops and stares; at ! he shouts and chases.
  • Break line of sight and suspicion cools: he searches where he last saw you, then gives up. You always get a second chance.
  • Rugs are shadow — only a guard almost on top of you can make you out there. Walking feet are heard: sneak near patrols.
  • Take the target from its gold case, grab gems for score, then reach the EXIT. Cameras sweep later galleries — their alarm summons every guard.

Finish a gallery to unlock the next. A ghost — never truly seen — earns the night's best bonus.
